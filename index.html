Land Surface Temperature at 10 m from Sentinel-2 (MALMÖ-LST)

Landsat measures surface temperature at about 100 m. We learn its link to Sentinel-2 reflectance and map Malmö at 10 m, for 30 dates between 2018 and 2024. Every model we tested can be put on the maps below.

Part of the project AI-driven Urban Heat and Health Analysis using Satellite Data for Climate-Resilient Planning, Malmö University with Malmö stad.

Compare models on one date

Pick a date, then for each map a model, its loss or training, its loss cell (U-Net models), its teacher weight (U-Net student only) and its inputs. Scroll or use + and − to zoom, drag to move: all maps follow. Hover to read the temperature at the same spot in every map.
